About

My research interests are robot learning and world models. I have worked on vision-language-action (VLA) models for robot manipulation, including fine-tuning and testing on a real robot. I’m currently looking for PhD opportunities in these areas.

Selected Projects

VLA for Real-Robot Manipulation

I fine-tuned a VLA model and deployed it on a 6-DoF RealMan ECO65 arm. I evaluated the policy on closed-loop pick-and-place tasks with different object positions, starting poses, and visual backgrounds. In these tests, the policy achieved a 75% success rate, about 15 percentage points above a behavior cloning baseline.

World Model Experiments in Isaac Lab

I ran some early experiments with world models in Isaac Lab.

Multimodal Retrieval for Scientific Figures

I built a retrieval system that uses figures, captions, and surrounding text to match scientific figures to natural-language queries. I created over 10,000 figure–text pairs for training.
